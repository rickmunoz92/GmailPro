Your email stays with Gmail

Gmail Pro changes the Gmail interface locally. It does not send message content, BCC preferences, or label names to the subscription service. Stripe handles Checkout; the extension receives subscription status. Preferences can sync through Google’s Chrome Sync service.

Made for English desktop Gmail in Chrome 118 or newer. Mac shortcuts require macOS. Gmail updates may require compatibility fixes. Native Gmail features—including its reading pane, Undo Send and composer—remain owned by Gmail.
