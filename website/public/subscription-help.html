Manage your subscription.

Your 7-day free trial

Both plans begin with 7 days free. Checkout collects payment details and shows when billing will begin. Cancel before your trial ends to avoid the first charge.

Cancel or update payment details

Use your saved license to open Stripe’s customer portal from this website.

Or open Gmail Pro and choose Manage subscription. Stripe’s customer portal lets you cancel renewal, update your payment method, and view invoices. Return to the extension and choose Refresh status afterward.

Canceling renewal keeps access through the paid period. Uninstalling the extension or deactivating a browser does not cancel your subscription.

Activate another browser

Website purchases show a license after Checkout is verified. Keep that tab open and save the key privately. Paste it into the extension’s Already subscribed? form.

Open Your license key in your activated Gmail Pro popup. Treat the key like a password: anyone with it can use and manage your subscription. Save it privately. In the other browser, open Gmail Pro, expand Already subscribed?, paste the key, and choose Activate this browser.

Lost your key?

Contact support using the email you entered at Checkout. We’ll verify purchase ownership before helping restore access. Do not put payment details or license keys in public messages.

Offline or failed payment

Paid access can use a previously verified status for up to 24 hours, ending sooner if the paid period expires. Reconnect and refresh status to verify renewal. If payment needs attention, use Manage subscription.
