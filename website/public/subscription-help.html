Gmail ProSupport

Free access. Existing purchase help.

Gmail Pro is currently free for everyone who meets the age and agreement requirements. No payment details, subscription or license are needed. There is no automatic charge later; a future paid plan would require a separate purchase.

The tools below are only for managing an earlier purchase. You can get help without enabling mail features or accepting new terms.

Cancel or update payment details

Use your saved license to open Stripe’s customer portal from this website.

Or open Gmail Pro and choose Manage subscription. Stripe’s customer portal lets you cancel renewal, update your payment method, and view invoices. Return to the extension and choose Refresh status afterward.

Canceling renewal keeps access through the paid period. Uninstalling the extension or deactivating a browser does not cancel your subscription.

Using another browser

Install the current free release and review its Terms and Privacy disclosure. No license activation is needed. If you have a saved license from an earlier purchase, retain it privately for billing management; anyone with it can access the associated portal.

Lost your key?

Contact support using the email you entered at Checkout. We’ll verify purchase ownership before helping restore access. Do not put payment details or license keys in public messages.

Free features and billing

The current free release does not depend on a payment or subscription status check. Earlier billing records and cancellation support remain available separately.
