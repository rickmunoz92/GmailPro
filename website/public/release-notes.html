Release notes

What’s changed in Gmail Pro and what’s coming next.

Upcoming

  1. 1.0.13Chrome Web Store review required

    Clearer highlights when dragging threads

    When you drag one thread, only that thread is highlighted. Use Ctrl-click, Command-click, or Shift-click to select and drag multiple threads together.

Chrome Web Store

  1. 1.0.12November 17, 2026

    Smoother dragging in busy inboxes

    Reduces the pause when you drag a thread toward a label, especially in inboxes with many labels.

  2. 1.0.11November 17, 2026

    Clearer thread wording and more reliable startup

    Mailbox counts and empty states now use “thread.” Apple Mail Mode also recognizes updated Gmail label menus and marks supported unread threads as read when you archive them.

  3. 1.0.8November 10, 2026

    Faster pickup when dragging threads

    Reduced the delay when you pick up a thread to drag it to a label.

  4. 1.0.7November 9, 2026

    More accurate recipient details

    Corrected To and CC grouping in the reading pane while preserving BCC. Gmail Pro also recovers more reliably if its first startup attempt is missed.

  5. 1.0.6November 9, 2026

    Smoother first-time setup

    Gmail Pro does a better job of respecting your reading pane choices during setup. It also keeps Gmail’s side panel toggle visible and matches inbox scrollbars to your theme.

  6. 1.0.5November 7, 2026

    First Chrome Web Store release

    Gmail Pro launched free with a cleaner Gmail layout, newest replies first, message zoom, custom label order, and optional Auto BCC.

  1. 0.3.1September 17, 2026

    First public source release

    The original Gmail Pro added optional Auto BCC and put the newest message at the top of open threads. View the 0.3.1 source.
