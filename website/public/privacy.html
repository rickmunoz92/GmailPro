Privacy policy

Effective October 29, 2026. Gmail Pro is operated by Rick Munoz, independently of Google and Apple.

Gmail data stays in Gmail

The extension processes Gmail page elements locally to provide the features you enable. This can include displayed conversation content, sender information, labels, account addresses, read status and recipient fields. Gmail Pro does not send email bodies, subjects, attachments, recipients, BCC preferences, label names or Gmail account addresses to its billing service. Gmail itself continues to process your mail and save drafts normally.

Preferences and your license

Feature switches, themes, the BCC address, label order, and account addresses paired with hidden sublabel paths use Chrome’s synchronized extension storage. If Chrome Sync is enabled, Google can sync those settings between your signed-in Chrome browsers. Subscription license keys, purchase tokens and recently verified subscription status use local extension storage restricted to trusted extension contexts, rather than Chrome Sync. Clearing extension data removes local activation; your saved license can restore it.

Payments and subscription verification

Stripe handles payment Checkout, customer billing information, receipts, invoices and subscription management under Stripe’s privacy policy. We do not receive full card numbers or card security codes. Our Netlify-hosted service sends Stripe the selected plan and a hashed random purchase token and reads subscription/payment status. Signed license keys refer to a Stripe subscription. Stripe subscription metadata records fulfillment, current status, and any refund or fraud restriction; Stripe retains the billing record. No separate mailbox account is created.

When a license is activated or checked, the extension sends the license key to our service over HTTPS. Active subscription status is normally cached for six hours; activation, manual refresh and troubleshooting may make additional checks. The last verified access can remain available for up to 24 hours and no longer than its trial or paid period. Your device stores that temporary status.

Website and support

Netlify hosts the website and billing service and may process IP addresses and routine request/security logs to deliver and protect them. A support request stores the email and message you choose to submit in Netlify Forms so we can respond. Do not send mailbox contents, passwords, full payment details or license keys through support. Support information is retained as needed to resolve requests and meet applicable obligations; request deletion through the support form.

No advertising or analytics

Gmail Pro includes no advertising trackers, behavioral analytics, or sale of personal information. Data is used to provide the extension, verify subscriptions, prevent abuse, and handle support. We do not use email content to train AI models or for advertising. The extension loads executable code bundled with its installation.

Your controls

Disable individual features, deactivate the current browser, or uninstall the extension at any time. Use Manage subscription to cancel renewal; uninstalling or clearing data does not cancel billing. Use Chrome’s settings to manage Chrome Sync. For access, correction or deletion requests concerning information we control, use the support form. Billing records may need to be retained by Stripe or us for accounting, dispute resolution or legal obligations.

Limited use

Gmail Pro’s use of information received through Chrome APIs follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Information is used only for the prominently described features, billing, security and support.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be reflected here with an updated effective date and any additional notice or consent required. Contact the operator through Gmail Pro support.
