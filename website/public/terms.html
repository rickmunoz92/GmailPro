Subscription terms

Effective October 29, 2026. Gmail Pro is an independent browser extension operated by Rick Munoz. It is not a Google or Apple product.

Price and renewal

Full access costs US$0.99 per month or US$10 per year. Your chosen interval renews automatically until canceled. Checkout shows the recurring amount, billing interval and any applicable taxes before you pay. Each new subscription starts with a 7-day free trial. Payment details are collected at Checkout. Cancel before the trial ends to avoid the first charge. The selected recurring price is charged after the trial unless canceled. Stripe processes payments.

Cancellation

Cancel renewal at any time using Manage subscription in the extension. Access continues through the paid period. Uninstalling, disabling features, or choosing Deactivate this browser does not cancel renewal. For help canceling or a refund request, use the support form with your Checkout email. Refund requests are reviewed individually; statutory cancellation and refund rights remain available.

Using your license

Keep your license key private. You can restore it in your own browsers. It is not for resale or public sharing. A valid subscription enables the paid Chrome Web Store distribution’s tools. Access can pause when payment fails, the paid period ends, a payment is fully refunded, or fraud/dispute review is required. Subscription management and support remain available.

Compatibility and updates

Gmail Pro targets English desktop Gmail in Chrome 118 or later. Mac keyboard shortcuts require macOS. Gmail’s interface and other extensions can affect compatibility, and fixes can require updates. Gmail remains responsible for your mailbox, native compose behavior and sending. Auto BCC is a convenience; always verify recipients before sending. Do not rely on it as a compliance or guaranteed delivery mechanism.
